CCR Solution

한 권의 고전이 화면이 되기까지

CCR(Cultural Classics Revitalization)은 고전 텍스트를 현대적 시청각 콘텐츠로 재탄생시키는 자체 솔루션입니다. 5단계 전 공정을 4개의 자체 AI 모듈이 운영하며, Google Cloud Run · Vertex AI 위에서 통합 작동합니다.

About

주식회사 노크유 · KNOCKU Inc.

KNOCKU Inc. is a Busan-based technology studio that automates the production of classical and humanities content. Our in-house CCR solution — four AI modules running on Google Cloud (Cloud Run · Vertex AI) — runs the full pipeline from source-text analysis to multilingual subtitling. Content produced on our platform has reached over 146 million views and a 1.5-million-strong subscriber audience, and we have delivered branded content with partners including government ministries, EBS, CJ ENM, NC SOFT, KT and major publishers. KNOCKU was founded in 2023 and is led by founder & CEO Yoo Soo Choi.
